# Glimway Lore & Magic: Memory, Workings & The Hungry Dark

*Unified design reference for Glimway's cosmology, tangible light, the creeping Dark, the Five Practices, and living history.*

---

## 1. The Core Cosmology: The Deep Measure & The Wounded Turning

Why do the mountains, lakes, and the River Wend stay put while the woods, paths, and ruins shift every week?

* **The Deep Measure (What Endures):** The bedrock of the world—its ridges, lake basins, and riverbeds—was laid down to a slow, ancient foundation called **the Deep Measure**. Stone and deep water are old enough and heavy enough to remember their own shape across all biomes.
* **The Turning (A Wounded Memory):** Everything above the bedrock—trees, reeds, footpaths, cottages, and local history—is lighter. In a healthy world, things only fade over centuries of neglect. In Glimway today, however, the world suffers from an unnatural, accelerated forgetting called **the Drift**. Every seven days (a **Wick**), anything that is not actively loved, walked, worked, or lit loosens from the present and slips back into *what-might-have-been*.
  * *Why is the world like this?* Because in the deep past, someone meddled with the Deep Measure to abolish human toil—and broke the world's ability to hold onto yesterday (see §5).
* **The Whitequiet (In an Endless World):** Across a near-endless map, the Whitequiet manifests in two ways:
  1. **The Unwalked Veil:** At the frontier of where anyone in the community has explored, the world waits in a pale, silent mist of unremembered potential until someone walks into it or lights a lamp, parting the veil to reveal new biomes and lands.
  2. **Bleached Hollows:** Where the Dark festers unchecked for too long, it drinks the color, warmth, and sound right out of a valley or marsh, creating localized pockets of Whitequiet that players can push back with light, song, and restoration.

## 2. Tangible Light, Honest Work & The Hungry Dark

In Glimway, both Light and Dark are **physical, tactile realities**, and entropy isn't fought by conquering armies—it is fought by **faithful, everyday work that dignifies life, creates beauty, and serves others**.

### Tangible Light (Glims, Sap & Footsteps)
* **What a "Glim" Is:** Light in Glimway has weight and warmth. It gathers in **glims**—embers heavy with earned light, kindled by *care*: keeping a promise, tending a duty, mending what is broken, or helping a neighbor. You can pour it, temper a chisel with it, or anchor a clearing with it.
* **The Wick Tree & Witnessing Light:** At the heart of Hearthwick grows the **Wick Tree**, whose roots drink from the Deep Measure. Its golden sap, refined into lamp oil, doesn't just make fire—it **witnesses** the land around it. While a lantern burns, the land beneath it is seen, remembered, and held in the present.
* **Desire Paths (Memory in Footsteps):** A road stays a road because friends walk it to visit each other or carry goods to market. Every footstep presses human presence into the soil, keeping common ways open across the Turning.
* **Beauty Creates Permanence:** Slapdash, joyless extraction fades quickly. Taking raw clay from Lake Country, shaping it with patience, and firing it in Aldo's kiln—or carving a beam, baking bread, weaving a rug, or composing a song—gives fleeting matter **lasting form and beauty**. The more care and skill poured into an object or home, the deeper its anchor in reality.

### The Hungry Dark (The Discord / The Unlit)
* **Awake, Personal, and Creepy:** The Dark beyond the lamplight is not peaceful night—it is **an ancient, calculating intelligence** with a single, consuming will: **to snuff out every light, unmake memory, sever community, and drag every living thing into cold, isolated silence**.
* **How It Operates:** It presses against the edge of lantern-glow like cold water against glass, and *it listens*. It hates beauty, rhythm, and kept promises because those things anchor the world against it. When a traveler is exhausted, proud, bitter, or greedy, the Dark whispers back—using familiar voices, old regrets, or promises of easy power to convince them that *work is pointless*, *nobody is coming to help you*, and *taking a shortcut is wisdom*.
* **Who Resists & Who Falls:**
  * **The Anchored:** People who love their neighbors, take pride in honest craft, tend their hearths, and create music or art have deep roots in reality. The Dark finds them heavy and almost impossible to twist.
  * **The Untethered:** Those who despise effort, hoard for themselves, or sink into bitter isolation have shallow roots. Some are easily infected; others willingly seek out the Dark's shortcuts so they can rule or take without working.

## 3. Magic as "Workings" & The Five Practices

You don't "cast spells" in Glimway; you perform **Workings** (or **Practices**)—working earned light into stone, spark, voice, hearth, or root through practice and refinement.

Whole, healthy Workings rest on three things working together: **Cadence** (the living breath, rhythm, or intent that wakes the magic), **Vessel / Rune** (the carved mark, tool, or form that shapes and holds it), and **Care / Glim** (the honest effort or ember that feeds it).

Every Practice gives a player both a **Quiet Craft** (town, building, making, economy) and a **Sharp Edge** (combat, delves, pushing back the Dark), so no single playstyle outshines another:

| Practice | How You Refine It | Quiet Craft (Town, Making & World) | Sharp Edge (Delves, Bosses & The Dark) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Handcraft** *(Runes & Stone)* | Shaping clay, wood, iron, and etching maker's marks. | Carves runes that hold land, reinforce bridges, and forge durable masterwork tools and building kits. | Drops **Rune-Anchors** mid-fight that block boss charges, crack dark constructs, and hold safe ground. |
| **2. Waycraft** *(Sparks & Signs)* | Braving the unknown, clearing trails, and fighting camps. | Reads the Turning, lights way-beacons, and senses where the Dark is pooling. | Fast **Reflex Workings** (*Fingersnap*, interrupts, blazing dashes through shadow hazards, drawing aggro). |
| **3. Songcraft** *(Cadence & Names)* | Restoring library poems/ballads, playing music, and keeping history. | Pieces lost papers into songs, wakes old shrines, and names lamps with lasting power. | Weaves **Countersongs** that strip a boss's dark frenzy, stagger Unmoored packs, and steady allies' nerves. |
| **4. Hearthcraft** *(Ember & Bond)* | Tending lamps, cooking/baking, hosting, and helping friends. | Co-signs Hearths, refines potent lamp oils, and crafts restorative provisions for delves. | Projects **Sanctuary**—splitting damage across friends, reviving downed allies, and burning back creeping shadow. |
| **5. Wildcraft** *(Root, Water & Beast)* | Fishing, raising pets, tending reeds/gardens, reading weather. | Companions sniff out buried caches; coaxes rare reeds/clay and reads deep waters. | Calls loyal beasts to harry foes and turns terrain (roots, mist, water) into snares against the Unmoored. |

## 4. Progression: Generalists vs. Specialists

As players practice these Workings, they earn **Practice Ranks**. Rather than locking players into rigid classes, they can spread out as a **Generalist** or go deep as a **Specialist**—with real pros and cons for both:

### The Generalist ("The Jack-of-All-Lanes")
* **How it works:** You level 3–5 Practices evenly up to the mid-tiers.
* **The Pros:**
  * **Complete Self-Sufficiency:** Ideal for solo sessions—you can hold your own in a delve, mend your own gear, sing a basic shrine awake, and tend your own camp.
  * **Cross-Workings (Hybrid Combos):** Reaching mid-rank in two different Practices unlocks clever combination tricks that pure specialists don't get on their own:
    * *Handcraft + Songcraft:* Carve **Resonant Stones** that hum a warning when the Dark approaches a road.
    * *Waycraft + Wildcraft:* **Shadow-Tracking**—your pet can follow the trail of an Unmoored elite or sniff out hidden $z = -1$ delve entrances.
    * *Hearthcraft + Waycraft:* **Blazing Trail**—sprint speed and warm regen for everyone walking a desire path you recently tended.
* **The Con:** You cannot reach the deepest **Masterwork Ranks** on your own—you rely on a Specialist friend (or their market stall / co-op help) for pinnacle crafts, deep rune-forging, or high-tier boss redemptions.

### The Specialist ("The Master Practitioner")
* **How it works:** You pour your focus deep into 1 (or 2) Practices to reach the highest **Masterwork Ranks**.
* **The Pros (Exclusive Pinnacle Capabilities):**
  * **Master Handcrafter:** Forges permanent Rune-Keystones, mends shattered ancient relics, and crafts high-tier building kits/weapons to sell at their market stall.
  * **Master Waycrafter:** Cuts straight through Bleached/Unlit hollows and lands lethal parry-counters on bosses.
  * **Master Songcrafter:** Speaks deep True Names that break a boss's corruption in the final phase or unlock sealed lore vaults.
  * **Master Hearthcrafter:** Brews ever-bright oil, masters deep tactile baking/cooking simulations, and holds a dome of light in the deepest $z = -1$ pitch-black caverns.
  * **Master Wildcrafter:** Bonds with rare apex companions and commands deep-water/weather phenomena.
* **The Con:** You rely on friends or the town market for the pillars you skipped—a pure Waycrafter/Delver buys their kiln-fired gear and baked provisions from a Maker's stall, paying them in rare delve loot and glims.

## 5. Overreach, The Breaking of the Measure & Two Generations of History

### How Good Workings Go Wrong (Overreach)
Because the Dark is intelligent and patient, it doesn't just attack with claws—it waits for someone to **force a good Working past honest care**:
* Every honest Working takes patience, presence, or shared effort. When someone tries to cheat that limit—cutting a coercive rune so they never have to tend a wall again (**Dead-Binding**), burning glims greedily for raw destruction (**Scorching**), using a True Name to puppet a creature (**Compulsion**), or locking a hearth inward out of selfish fear (**Hoarding**)—the Working **cracks**.
* **The Infection:** The Dark slips straight into that crack, siphoning warmth out of the surrounding land and slowly hollowing out the worker. That temptation is what people call **the Old Ways**.

### Deep History: The Breaking of the Measure
* Long ago, a brilliant arch-artisan grew resentful of the slow, patient rhythm of human work—of ploughing, mending, walking, and tending lamps.
* Whispered to by the Dark, they sought to "free" humanity from toil forever by carving vast, coercive **Binding Runes** directly into the bedrock of the Deep Measure, trying to force the earth to yield harvests, light, and permanence automatically without human care or love.
* **The Catastrophe:** You cannot harvest light without care. The forced runes cracked the world's foundation. Instead of eternal effortless stasis, the world's memory shattered into the seven-day **Turning**—and the crack in the Deep Measure let the Dark pour into the world. *(End-game horizon: One day, players may uncover those original cracked Great Runes in the deep earth and begin truly healing the world's memory.)*

### Recent History (1–2 Generations Ago): The Fracturing of the Road
Before the players arrived in Hearthwick, a golden effort rose a generation or two ago—**the Company of the Road** (or *the Wardens of the Span*)—including the parents and mentors of Hearthwick's older residents. They tried to relight the Lantern Road, maintain outposts like **Brackenwood** and **Ashwatch**, and hold the river crossings.
* **How They Broke Apart:** As the Dark pressed harder and sowed distrust, the Company fractured. Some grew tired of doing their share; others turned to forbidden shortcut runes out of desperation; others retreated behind locked doors when the crossings fell.
* **Where They Are Now:**
  * **The Lonely Holdouts (NPCs & Outposts):** A few aging survivors still hold out alone in remote corners of the Wilds—exhausted, protective, and wary, waiting to see if the new generation in Hearthwick will actually keep their word.
  * **The Unmoored Beasts:** Simple wolves, beetles, and marsh-creatures keep their animal nature, but the Dark directs their fear and hunger against lamplight. Breaking their fever in combat frees them to flee back into the natural woods.
  * **The Fallen Wardens (Intelligent Bosses):** Skilled wardens and artisans—like those who guarded **Dorrit's Span** or **Sallow Ford**—succumbed to the Dark's whispers when they were abandoned or overreached with Binding Runes. They retain their cunning, craft, and Workings, now twisted toward hoarding glims and hunting anyone carrying a lantern.
  * **The 52 Library Papers:** Scattered through the library and ruins are the **work-songs, travel poems, letters, and rune-sketches** left behind by these real families and lineages.

## 6. Playstyle Choices: Delvers, Looters, Builders & Story-Lovers

Different friends want different payoffs, and how they handle the world's threats leaves permanent, contrasting marks on their server:

### Example: The Fallen Warden at Sallow Ford (Elara's Mother)
When the old Company fractured, Elara's mother tried to hold Sallow Ford alone. Exhausted and abandoned, she listened to the Dark's whisper in the river and cut a forbidden Binding Rune to hold the ford forever—becoming a terrifying, intelligent Unmoored boss.

1. **The Delver & Looter Path (Conquest / Combat Focus):**
   * **What you do:** March in with your combat/Waycraft gear and defeat her head-on at full strength.
   * **The Payoff:** She drops **Warden's Dark-Iron Gear** and rare delve trophies. Because her forced rune was shattered by violence rather than unwound, the Dark bleeds down into the $z = -1$ cavern beneath the ford—permanently opening a **repeatable High-Danger Delve** where fighters and looters can farm rare materials and boss rematches each wick.
2. **The Chronicler & Maker Path (Redemption / Multi-Pillar Co-op):**
   * **What you do:** Uncover her old letters (Waycraft), mend her cracked lantern at the kiln (Handcraft), piece together her family ballad from library papers (Songcraft), and shield the singer during the fight's final phase (Hearthcraft).
   * **The Payoff:** The Dark's hold shatters without killing her. She remembers her name, reconciles with Elara, and becomes a **permanent Outpost Keeper**, teaching Masterwork recipes/songs and keeping Sallow Ford safely lit.
3. **Working Together:** In a mixed friend group, a **Delver** can lead the vanguard and break her armor phase so the **Maker** and **Story-Lover** can complete the redemption—or the group can choose to turn one ruin into a repeatable farming Delve while redeeming the next Warden for the town.
