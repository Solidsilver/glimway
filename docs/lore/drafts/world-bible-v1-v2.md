# Fingersnap World Bible (Version 2)

## 0. The Pitch
Thirty years ago, during the lull between the Two Storms, six people from Hearthwick went out under the closure mark to fetch lamp oil so their keeper could reopen the road. Two died in the shifting Tangle. Four reached the far town of Tarrow-on-the-Reed. The keeper learned they survived when a Count House receipt made its way back, but she kept it secret so others wouldn't risk the deadly, shifting Wilds to find them. The Lost Expedition stops being a mystery of "what ate them?" and becomes "what do you do with news that is thirty years late?" The player's arrival makes the road holdable again, and every lantern lit removes a little more of the reason the keeper stayed silent. 

## Resolved Conflicts
- **The Keeper's Name:** Wenna Hale (Opus).
- **The Survivors' Fate vs. The Count House:** The survivors settled at Tarrow-on-the-Reed (Opus), rather than being trapped in a surreal thirty-year commute (Mimo). However, Wenna learned of their survival through the Count House receipts (Mimo) that eventually made their way up the hill road, proving the carters had punched their tallies at the far end.
- **The Far Town:** Tarrow-on-the-Reed (Mimo) is the far end of the road. 
- **Echoes:** They are drift-preserved impressions that surface at season turnings and are "settled" with owed lamps (GLM), grounding them in the world's mechanics rather than being literal ghosts.

## 1. World Rules (The Strange)
- **The Drift:** The land forgets what it isn't reminded of. Unanchored land drifts slowly; during storms or turnings, it moves in minutes (a "stir"). 
- **Anchor-Light:** Anchoring is memory, not magic. It requires three things: **amber-oil** (the Wilds' congealed memory), a **naming** spoken by the keeper ("You are the third turning..."), and the **lamp** itself. 
- **Hollow Lights:** A lantern with oil but no naming burns hollow. It warms, but it anchors nothing.
- **The Stone Warden:** Built by Wenna and Orrin using drift-stone and an amber heart-lamp. It was given one naming: "The road is closed here." Because it is named land, it obeys the drift rule—it moves only when unobserved, always stepping back into the gap to fulfill its instruction. It is not an ancient golem testing worth; it is a heavy sentence with a light in it. Showing it the closure rubbing amends the instruction.

## 2. History Timeline
- **The Settling (~155 years ago):** Hearthwick founded for iron-oak and amber. 
- **The Lantern Road (~110 years ago):** Carter guilds and keepers build the chain of lamps to Tarrow-on-the-Reed to hold the shifting track still.
- **The Weir Years (~36 years ago):** Trade peaks. The road is pinned straighter and longer than the drift likes. Pressure builds like water behind a weir. 
- **Winter of Two Storms (31 years ago):** 
  - *First Storm:* The drift turns violent. Wenna closes the road (two weaves and a break). 
  - *The Lull:* The Six refuse the closure and push out to get oil. Silas turns back.
  - *Second Storm (The Stir):* The Tangle folds. Orrin's bridge snaps. Two die; four push through to Tarrow.
- **Aftermath (30 years ago):** Searchers go in. Wenna and Orrin build the Ashwatch warden to stop anyone from following. The Count House receipt arrives; Wenna realizes four lived, but keeps the secret.
- **The Lean Wicks (30-5 years ago):** Trade shrinks to the hill road. The square lamp goes out.
- **Now:** The player arrives. The drift is calmer. The road can be remembered again.

## 3. The Map Beyond the Map
- **Tarrow-on-the-Reed:** The far river market town (nine days by the hill road). Home of the Count House (where tolls and tallies are kept) and the Oak Hall.
- **Sennick's Ferry:** A chain-ferry across the Reed. The Sennicks cut the chain and move the ferry when the river drifts. 
- **The Oaker Hills / The Tops:** Charcoal burners and smallholdings over the ridge. The hill road is Hearthwick's only remaining outside contact.
- **The Amberwash:** Pale gravel scars where amber crews harvest storm-grade amber.
- **The Merrow Saltings:** Fen country far south-west, where people farm the drift like a tide. Elara's home.

## 4. The Lost Expedition
**What really happened:** The Lull Run. Hollis, Tam, Bett, Dorrit, Joss, and Nan pushed into the Tangle. Bett and Dorrit died when the bridge drifted out. The other four reached Tarrow. Silas (Hollis's husband) was meant to go, but turned back because he was afraid, blaming his knee.

**The Six:**
1. **Hollis Brack (41 then):** Lead carter. Survived. Keeps a lantern lit at Tarrow's end.
2. **Tam Cooley (34 then):** Ox-driver. Survived.
3. **Bett Cooley:** Tam's twin. Lost at the Tangle crossing.
4. **Dorrit Venn (20 then):** Orrin's apprentice. Lost at the crossing.
5. **Joss Penhallow (16 then):** Runner. Survived. Still trying to chart a way back.
6. **Nan Greer (50s then):** Lamplighter. Survived. Died in Tarrow eleven years ago.

**The Reveal Ladder:**
1. **Quests:** The road was closed on purpose. The Warden was village-built.
2. **POIs (Inner):** The Cooley Cart, the Frozen Pond (Dorrit's glove) — the Six were real people.
3. **Trinkets:** Things surfacing that were meant to come back (waxed bootlaces, unlit candles).
4. **Echoes:** Finding drift-impressions of the Six at camp, showing they didn't flee in panic.
5. **Seasons / Outer POIs:** Turncaps face east toward Tarrow. Joss's notches point *home*. 
6. **Journal / Ledger:** The last page of Wenna's ledger isn't a memorial, it's headed "Owing"—a standing order to keep an account open.
7. **Endgame Image:** The Keeper's Light project finishes. The player sees a second point of gold on the far horizon—Hollis's lantern, waiting.

## 5. The People
**Mara Hale (27) — Keeper of the Ledger**
- **Backstory:** Wenna's granddaughter, raised in the Keeper's House.
- **Want:** For the road to be lit as Wenna's road, restoring her grandmother's legacy.
- **Secret/Fear:** She holds the last page of the ledger in keeper's marks she can't read, terrified it proves Wenna did something wrong. 
- **Relationships:** Protective of the village; bickers fondly with Orrin.
- **Voice:** Brisk, warm, lists things in threes. 
- **Samples:** 
  - "Twelve casks of oil, four of barley, one of whatever Orrin calls varnish. Noted, noted, and I'd rather not."
  - "Soup's on the ledger. Leek and barley. If Orrin tells you it needs salt, it doesn't."

**Pip Penhallow (12) — Runner**
- **Backstory:** Hazel the baker's child, paid in Silas's whittled foxes.
- **Want:** To map the shifting Wilds (the "Pencil Map") and be a real runner.
- **Secret/Fear:** They sneak into the Tangle at dawn to track notches. Hazel is terrified of the dark, not Pip. 
- **Relationships:** Hazel (protective mother), Silas (fox supplier). Joss was their uncle.
- **Voice:** Bursts of enthusiasm, counts things, grand words used slightly wrong.
- **Samples:** 
  - "I counted the lanterns. Two and a firefly. Two and a half. I'm rounding up for morale."
  - "This is the Pencil Map. Pencil because the Tangle moves. It's not wrong, it's historical."

**Orrin (sixty-odd) — Bridge Builder**
- **Backstory:** Built the warden with Wenna. Dorrit was his apprentice. 
- **Want:** Things that stay where they're put.
- **Secret/Fear:** The signpost leans because Dorrit set it crooked on her first day; he resets it to the same lean every year.
- **Relationships:** Respects Mara, 30-year unspoken tension with Silas (who turned back).
- **Voice:** Short declaratives. Complains, then concedes. 
- **Samples:** 
  - "Signpost's three fingers off plumb. Frost. Don't look at me like that. I said frost."
  - "Hesper named it. I carved it. Worst joint I ever made. It's the only one that's never leaned."

**Silas (64) — Retired Carter / Builder**
- **Backstory:** Hollis's husband. Drove the road.
- **Want:** To build homes for other people since he's lost his own.
- **Secret/Fear:** He wasn't injured; he turned back at the Arch out of fear. He feels immense survivor's guilt.
- **Relationships:** Orrin (tension), Pip (lets them watch him carve door-foxes). 
- **Voice:** Slow, measured. Uses cart metaphors for houses.
- **Samples:** 
  - "A house is just a box until someone lights a fire in it. Let's get your hearth stones laid."
  - "The knee was fine. It's always been fine. I heard the wind turn at the fork, and I was scared, and Hollis wasn't."

**Elara Quill (30s) — Forager (Merrow Saltings)**
- **Backstory:** Drift-forager carried away by a spring flood-drift. Set up camp in Hearthwick Commons.
- **Want:** To prove the drift has tides; secretly, to find a way home without admitting she's lost.
- **Secret/Fear:** She knows her sister keeps a lamp lit for her.
- **Relationships:** Argues with Finn about the river's timetable.
- **Voice:** Precise, analytical, uses Saltings terms ("on the flood", "slack water").
- **Samples:** 
  - "Don't step on the thread. That's three weeks of drift on one string. You're a colleague now. Hold this jar."
  - "Your village nails the tablecloth down with lanterns. Where I'm from we just learn when it's going to be pulled."

**Finn Tolley (36) — Miller**
- **Backstory:** Aldo's son. Inherited the wandering river Reed. 
- **Want:** A river that stays put. To be let off the promise of keeping the box of Count House tokens his father hid.
- **Secret/Fear:** He holds the evidence that the carters reached Tarrow. 
- **Relationships:** Elara (argues about drift timetables).
- **Voice:** Anxious, numerate, apologetic run-ons.
- **Samples:** 
  - "The Wend wandered three feet west last night. The wheel's groaning. Thirty-eight and complaining."
  - "Sorry. Sorry, I'm counting. If I stop counting it gets worse. It doesn't, actually. But it feels like it will."

## 6. Calendar, Festivals, Sayings, and Songs
**The Calendar (Wicks and Marks):**
- **Wicks (months):** Thaw, Mud, Bud, Bloom, Light, Cart, Haze, Sap, Amber, Leaf, Smoke, Quiet.
- **Marks (seasons):** Mudrise (spring thaw), Carting (summer), Amberfall (autumn harvest), Quiet (winter deep dark). 

**The Turning:** The outer Wilds shift on a schedule (e.g., "Dark of Amber-wick"). Posted by Elara on the notice board. 

**Festivals:**
- **The Breaking:** Ice-out on the Reed. Children float candle hulls.
- **Carting Day:** A Commons market. An empty horse collar (hame) is hung on the gate for the cart that never came home.
- **Amberwake:** Autumn festival. Window lamps lit with fresh amber glass. An Empty Chair is set at the Long Table.
- **Closure Night:** Winter anniversary of the closing. Every village lantern is trimmed; the road past the gate is purposefully left dark.

**Sayings:**
- *"Two weaves and a break."* A decision made and kept.
- *"Stealing shade."* Taking unmarked iron-oak (stealing from grandchildren).
- *"Leave one for Ada."* Leave a light on for someone who is late (Ada kept a lamp for her twins for 30 years).
- *"Walk by stone, not by path."* Paths lie; cairns are honest.

**Songs:**
- **The Carters' Counting Song:** A mnemonic map ("One for the gate and two for the hill...").
- **Leave the Last Lantern:** A lullaby sung at Closure Night.

## 7. How Story Reaches the Player
- **Dialogue:** Season-marked lines (Orrin complains about frost heave in Mudrise). Evolving terms of address (nodding terms → tea terms → spare-key terms).
- **Journal:** The player connects clues (e.g., matching the Count House token to the timeline).
- **Notice Board:** Posts announce Turnings, village projects, and small ads. Signatures reveal NPC dynamics.
- **Echoes (The Wilds):** Turnings surface "Echoes"—memories of the Six. Players spend embers to "settle" them with owed lamps.
- **Trinkets & POIs:** Two-stage blurbs. Finding a trinket gives basic lore; witnessing its Echo updates the blurb with the owner's name.
- **Homestead Visits:** NPCs visit at new tiers, dropping lore (e.g., Silas brings a door-fox at Tier 1, revealing carter customs).
- **Projects:** e.g., "The Keeper's Light" project restores the lamp-loft, unlocking Wenna's primer so Mara can read the last page.

## 8. Tone Guide
**Do's:**
- Emphasize memory and bookkeeping over mysticism. Magic is just the land remembering. 
- Make danger mild and defeat a stumble (you wake up having learned something).
- Keep the world tactile (smell of woodsmoke, weight of amber, sound of the mill wheel).

**Don'ts:**
- No "Light vs. Dark" as Good vs. Evil. Lit = remembered; dark = unremembered/fluid. The Wilds aren't evil, just unsettled.
- No chosen ones, dark lords, or ancient destinies. 
- Never mention real-life Habitica tasks in-world (use embers/warmth).

## 9. Ideas Parked
- **The "Account Still Open" Commute:** Mimo suggested the Six are literally commuting through a 30-year space-time drift anomaly, continually showing up at Tarrow. It's a fun idea, but slightly too surreal. We parked it in favor of Opus's grounded bittersweet ending: they reached Tarrow and lived their lives there, and the Count House receipts proved it.
