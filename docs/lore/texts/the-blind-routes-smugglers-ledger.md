# The "Blind Routes" (Fragment of a Smuggler's Ledger)

**Author:** Unknown (Likely a rogue amber-crew captain or a disgraced Lamplighter).
**Location Found:** Hidden inside a hollowed-out log near the Sallow Ford ferry ruins.
**Date:** Unknown, but the ink predates the Two Storms.
**Material:** Tiny, cramped handwriting in a pocket-sized notebook. Several pages are torn out.

*(...page begins mid-sentence...)*
...cannot afford the Oak Hall's wheel-tax anymore. Two coppers a league is robbery. The Count Post sits on the only anchored road, but if you are willing to walk the Wilds in the dark, you can bypass the toll entirely. We call them the Blind Routes.

**Rule 1: Borrow the Light.**
Do not light your own lantern. The Wardens watch for moving light. Instead, wait for the slack water of the Green Hush. Use the "turncaps" (the grey mushrooms). They always tilt toward the nearest official lantern on the main road. If you keep the tilt of the caps exactly at a 90-degree angle to your left shoulder, you are walking parallel to the road, safely hidden in the dark, but close enough to avoid the worst of the deep drift.

**Rule 2: The Amberwash Trick.**
The tollmen know the paths, so you must use the water. The Amberwash gravel is loose. If the Wilds shift, the gravel just shuffles; it rarely folds or snaps like the forest floor. Walk in the dry streambeds. 

**Rule 3: The Toll of the Dark.**
You save your silver, but the dark takes its own tax. Last week, we walked the Blind Route past Ashwatch. We didn't lose any amber, but Rennick forgot the name of his first dog, and I lost three days of memory from my childhood. The Wilds will pull the memory out of your head if you don't give it a flame to look at. 

Walk fast. Keep your mind on the tally. If you stop to rest in the unanchored dark, the woods won't kill you—they'll just quietly erase the reason you were traveling in the first place.

*(...a second leaf, folded small and wedged into the spine. Same hand. More hurried.)*

—and a fourth rule, for whoever carries this book after me. The first three only get you there.

**Rule 4: Carry the Reason Outside Your Head.**
The dark takes the errand before it takes the man. Before you step onto a route, write in tar on the inside of your wrist what you are doing, and who is waiting for you to come back. Say it aloud at every third turncap. Ink is a small anchor, and a cheap one. The Wardens never learned that. They think light is the only rope in the world, and they charge like it is the last one.

*(the leaf ends mid-word, as if the writer was interrupted—or forgot what came next)*

*From "Blood, Ink, and Timber: Legal and Architectural Records of the Reaches"*
