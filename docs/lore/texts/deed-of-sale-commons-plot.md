# Deed of Sale for a Commons Plot (Hearthwick)

**Date:** Year 142
**Material:** A standard bill of sale, filled out in Keeper Wenna’s neat handwriting.

**Transfer of Stewardship:**
Let it be recorded that Silas of the Carters' Compact has purchased the rights to build upon the eastern edge of the Hearthwick Commons. 

**Boundary Definition:**
Because the Commons edge borders the inner Wilds, this plot cannot be measured in yards. The boundaries of Silas’s property shall be defined as follows: 
From the western hinge of his front door, extending outward to the exact distance that a single, standard hearth-grade lantern casts its light on a moonless night in the season of the White Quiet. 

If Silas wishes a larger yard, he must afford better oil. If he lets his lamp go out, the yard belongs to the woods.

**Windfall Clause:**
Should the Wilds, in one of their turns, lay a tree across the yard, the tree is Silas's by windfall-right and free to take. He will leave three leafy branches at the stump, as the custom runs, so the next woodcutter knows the fall was no axe's doing, and he will not grudge the ground that did the laying.

*In the margin beside the last line, in the same neat hand: "Noted. — W.H." Beneath it, smaller, as if to herself: "The woods have never handed back a yard. Buy better oil than you think you need, carter."*

*From "Blood, Ink, and Timber: Legal and Architectural Records of the Reaches"*
