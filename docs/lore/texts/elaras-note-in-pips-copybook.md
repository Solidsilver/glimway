# Elara’s Note Found in Pip’s Copybook

*Written in neat, adult handwriting beneath a child's drawing of a walking hill.*

Pip, 
Your mother told you the story of 'The Hill That Swallowed the Greedy Forager' to stop you from eating berries before they are washed. 

Hills do not have mouths. They do not eat children who fail to wash their hands. 

However, if a hill is exhibiting a Fast Drift (look for the soil churning like boiling porridge and the smell of bruised roots), it will absolutely subduct you into the topsoil. It is not malicious; you are simply in the way of its geology. 

Wash your berries because foxes walk on them. Avoid moving hills because of subduction. 

Sincerely, 
E. Quill

*(Beneath the signature, in the same hand:)*

"P.S. A drift-table is only paper that remembers on purpose. When the flood brings my own light up the road, I will fold you one—in exchange for the proper note for that whistle of yours. Pencil only, Pip. The ground forgets. Paper should not."

*From "Bedtime Stories and Cautionary Tales of the Reaches"*
