# An Invoice from the Guild of Cartographers

**Author:** Master Cartographer Hemlock
**Date:** Last Autumn.
**Material:** A stiff, formal invoice sent to the Count House in Tarrow.

**BILL TO:** The Magistrates of the Oak Hall

**SERVICES RENDERED:**
- One (1) Standardized Map of the River Reed from Tarrow to the Brackenwood edge.
- Three (3) re-drawings of the Sennick Ferry crossing (due to the river moving the ferry).
- Five (5) re-drawings of the Amberwash shoals.
- Eleven (11) pencil amendments to the above, at the client's insistence, all rendered stale by the Leaf turn. No charge, the fault lying with the guild for inking them at all.

**TOTAL DUE:** 40 Silver Weights.

**ATTACHED NOTE:**
Do not ask my guild to map the Eastern Reaches again.
We map to claim ownership. We draw borders to say "this is ours." But the Drift rejects claims. To map the Wilds is to take a portrait of a stranger walking past your window and insist they will stand in that exact spot forever.

Pay the invoice, Magistrate. And the next time you need to know where the Brackenwood is, ask a woodcutter to point. It will be more accurate than my parchment.

*(On the reverse, in a magistrate's blunt hand:)*
PAID — 40 silver weights, drawn on the Count House.
For the record: we took your advice. A woodcutter pointed at the Brackenwood from the north gate, and he was right for a day and a half, which this Hall notes is the finest accuracy on file.
The next invoice will be settled sooner if it refrains from poetry. — For the Oak Hall

*From "The Liar's Art: Cartography and Exploration in the Reaches"*
