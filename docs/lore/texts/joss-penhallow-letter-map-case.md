# A Letter Kept in a Map Case

**Author:** Joss Penhallow
**Date:** Four years ago (Year 27 of the Closure)
**Material:** Oiled drafting vellum, folded many times. Found in a mapmaker's desk in Sallow Ford. Never sent.

Hazel,

I am forty-two today. I think. Nan used to keep the calendar for us, reading the wicks from how the sallow trees dropped their leaves, but Nan passed three winters gone. It’s just me, Tam, and Hollis now. 

I draw maps for Sallow Ford. Scientific cartography, I call it, though it’s mostly just drawing the same river three different ways depending on what mood the Wilds are in. 

I’ve been mapping the western edge of the Sallow woods. I found one of Nan’s old chalk marks yesterday, under a ridge overhang where the rain couldn't reach. A direction weave, pointing west. Pointing toward your bakery. I stood there for an hour, trying to remember the smell of the Twists. I can remember the honey, but I can't quite remember the salt. 

Hollis keeps the lamp lit on the hill here. He says someone in Hearthwick is leaving a light on for Ada, so he’s leaving one on for Silas. I didn't have the heart to tell him a lantern can't pierce thirty years of overgrown iron-oak. But I let him keep lighting it. 

I still have the whistle. It still plays the same note. If I blow it hard enough on a west wind, do you think Pip could hear it? Tell Pip... tell them runners don't stop. They just take the long route.

Love,
Joss

*P.S.* Nan's tally-stick is in my mapping drawer now. She used to cut one notch every Closure Night, and I never thought to ask what we were counting. Twenty-seven as of this week; I cut them myself these days. I'll need a fresh stick within the year, and I have decided not to think about that. If the stick ever comes back west, keep cutting it. Tell Pip it's arithmetic, not a wand.

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts, Vol. II"*
