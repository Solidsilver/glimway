# Excerpts from the Ashwatch Ledger

**Author:** Wenna Hale, Keeper of the Lantern Road
**Date:** The Year of the Two Storms (Thirty-one years ago)
**Material:** Green oilcloth ledger, written in iron-gall ink. 

*From the pages leading up to the Closure:*

**Amber-wick, 3rd:**
Four pints storm-grade cut with tallow. The Brackenwood stretch is drinking oil. Nan reports the third lantern past the arch burned hollow by midnight. The wick was fine, the oil was pure, but the land pulled so hard the flame just... forgot what it was holding. I re-named the stretch myself at dawn. *You are the ridge path. To your left the deep drop, to your right the oak stand.* It held, but the glass was hot enough to blister my thumb. The woods are pressing against the road like water against a dam. 

**Amber-wick, 14th:**
The Guild wants more lit-night running. Rennick sent a rider up from the Commons demanding we space the trimmings closer. They want the road straight and they want it fast. *Noted.* But you cannot pin a struggling animal to a board and expect it not to thrash. We are over-anchoring. 

**Amber-wick, 27th:**
The Turning came on schedule, and the third bowshot cairn wandered twice in one night. Nan walked the whole chain relighting by herself and came back with her hands steady on the taper and not steady after. I ordered her to sleep. She says the road does not sleep, so neither does she. *Noted, and overruled.* I will walk her stretch tomorrow, and she will hate me for it.

**Leaf-wick, 2nd:**
Oil, 3 casks short. The storm took the Sallow Ford train off the timetable entirely. Nan is exhausted. We are burning our reserves just to keep the stones from walking off the hills. 

**Leaf-wick, 4th:**
Road closed under the mark. Two weaves and a break, cut into the Ashwatch stone. Forgive the arithmetic, but I cannot spend what we do not have, and I will not let carters walk onto a road I cannot keep. Hollis Brack shouted himself hoarse in the square. Said they only need nine days to make a lull run for oil. I said no. *Noted.*

**Leaf-wick, 10th:**
Five days of slack calm, and Hollis has counted them like a miser counts coin. I told him the ninth day is not a promise; it is a guess. At dawn they went under the mark at the fork anyway. Nan would not meet my eye at the shrine. She read the mark twice, the way she reads, and went out with the rest. The wind changed before noon. *Noted.* Let the arithmetic be wrong. Let them come home and be wrong about the wind.

*(Three pages later, after the storms, written in a shaky, uncharacteristically messy hand)*

**Thaw-wick, 1st:**
Built a stubborn thing with Orrin. Eleven days. Drift-stone and Dorrit’s bridge timbers. My storm-grade amber in its chest. May it be more stubborn than the next fool. May the next fool not be Silas. 

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts of Hearthwick and the Lantern Road"*
