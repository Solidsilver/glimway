# Bett's Flat Verse

**Author:** Bett Cooley
**Date:** Thirty-one years ago (sung), settled today.
**Material:** An echo in the deep Wilds, finally resolved into memory.

*(A phantom voice, singing slightly off-key to the rhythm of a walking ox)*

Oh, the wheel goes round and the mud comes up,
And the Keeper pours from a bitter cup.
The road is long and the lantern's low,
And Tam is as slow as the grass can grow.

*(A pause, filled with the ghostly sound of an ox snorting and a man sighing in annoyance)*

I know I'm flat, Tam! I'm singing it flat so you'll walk faster to get away from the sound of it!

*(The voice resumes, catching the true note at last as the owed lamp flares to life)*

But the wick still draws and the flame still leaps,
And the amber holds while the forest sleeps.
So pull on the yoke and steady the load,
For we're the last ones on the Lantern Road.

*From "Bedtime Stories and Cautionary Tales of the Reaches"*
