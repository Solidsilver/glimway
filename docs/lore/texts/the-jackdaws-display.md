# The Jackdaw's Display

**Author:** Elara Quill
**Date:** Leaf-wick, this year.
**Material:** Field-note page, torn at the edge, smelling faintly of damp soil.

*Nest Inventory, Sector 7 (The Whitequiet)*

The corvids here do not collect shiny things. They collect things that are trying to go home.

Found a jackdaw's nest built entirely of dry grass and drift-twigs, anchored in the crotch of a dead iron-oak. Inside, there was no egg. There was only a display.

Item one: A leather work glove, right hand, sized for someone small. The index finger is stained with what looks like axle grease.
Item two: A beeswax candle, unlit. The wick is still pristine. Someone tied a bit of red yarn around the middle of it, the way they do for birthdays up in the village.
Item three: Eleven stamped road-nails. I checked the marks. They are heavy Tarrow iron, the kind the Compact stamped for pinning the iron-oak corduroy of the Lantern Road.

The Whitequiet spits out what it cannot digest. These aren't just lost things. The woods are keeping them pristine. The woods are holding their breath. Someone dropped these out there in the dark, and the land is simply waiting for them to walk back and pick them up.

*From "Scholarly Texts of the Reaches: Science, Taxonomy, and Misunderstanding"*
