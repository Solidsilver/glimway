# Tarrow Count House Requisition Reply

**Author:** Junior Clerk Vane, Tarrow Count House
**Date:** Twelve years ago.
**Material:** Stiff, cream-colored parchment bearing the Wheel and Wave seal.

**TO:** Master Orrin, Builder of Bridges, Hearthwick
**FROM:** The Oak Hall, Tarrow-on-the-Reed

Master Builder,

We are in receipt of your letter regarding the "hall price of hinges," as well as your colorful description of our clerks' parentage. 

While the Hall sympathizes with Hearthwick's isolation, the price of ironwork remains fixed. The cost of forging has risen due to the cessation of iron-oak shipments from the Brackenwood sector. As you are well aware, the Lantern Road has been defunct for nearly two decades following the loss of the Brack Convoy (Account 404, still open). 

If Hearthwick wishes to see the price of hinges fall, Hearthwick must supply the oak to fuel the forges. 

Furthermore, your requisition for three sets of brass plumb bobs is denied. We do not extend credit on account of "sentiment."

May the Reed flow true,
*Vane*

*A second note, smaller, in Vane's close clerical hand:*
**You will further observe that Account 404 remains open, and may not be offset, compounded, or closed while its tally stands unsettled. Any oak supplied by Hearthwick will be entered under a fresh account, not against the Convoy's. The Hall does not close an account merely because the debtors have stopped writing. Some accounts, we find, come in to settle themselves. — V.**

*Handwritten note at the bottom, in Orrin's heavy charcoal:*
**May the Reed flood your archives, Vane. I'll make the hinges from scrap.**

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts, Vol. II"*
