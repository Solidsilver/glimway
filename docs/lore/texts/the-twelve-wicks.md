# The Twelve Wicks: A Primer

**Author:** Traditional
**Date:** Unknown.
**Material:** A children's primer, pressed from the twelve painted blocks kept in the Commons meeting hall. This copy lives in the Keepers' reading room, where Hearthwick children learn their wicks.

First comes **Thaw**, the river breaks,
Then comes **Mud**, the wagon shakes.
Third is **Bud**, the branches green,
Fourth is **Bloom**, the prettiest seen.

Fifth is **Light**, the longest day,
Sixth is **Cart**, we haul away!
Seventh **Haze**, the summer heat,
Eighth is **Sap**, sticky and sweet.

Ninth is **Amber**, gold and bright,
Tenth is **Leaf**, the autumn's flight.
Eleventh **Smoke**, the chimneys blow,
Twelfth is **Quiet**, under the snow.

Twelve wicks burning in a line,
Keep them lit and we'll be fine!

*From "Bedtime Stories and Cautionary Tales of the Reaches"*
