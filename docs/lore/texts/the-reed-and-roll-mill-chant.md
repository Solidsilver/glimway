# Song: The Reed and Roll (Mill-hand's Chant)

**Author:** Traditional (Tolley Family)
**Date:** Unknown, pre-dates the Lantern Road.
**Material:** Oral tradition.

*(To be sung to the rhythm of a slow, grinding millstone. The caller sings the first line, the workers shout the response.)*

**Caller:** The water comes down from the Amberwash cold!
**All:** *Mind the wheel and mind the toe!*
**Caller:** And the stones in the dark are a sight to behold!
**All:** *Mind the wheel and mind the toe!*

**Caller:** Oh, the Reed will wander where the Reed will go!
**All:** *Heave the sack and let it blow!*
**Caller:** It was here yesterday but it’s moving below!
**All:** *Heave the sack and let it blow!*

**Caller:** And the Wend brings down what the first frost throws!
**All:** *Mind the wheel and mind the toe!*
**Caller:** So mind the grate, lad, wherever it goes!
**All:** *Mind the wheel and mind the toe!*

**Caller:** Stone that is steady and water that’s free!
**All:** *Grind the chaff for you and me!*
**Caller:** The miller stands between them, exactly like me!
**All:** *Grind the chaff for you and me!*

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts of Hearthwick and the Lantern Road"*
