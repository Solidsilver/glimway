# Last Will and Testament of Elias Fenn (Sapping-Grounds Worker)

**Dictated to:** Clerk Vane, Count Post Waystation
**Date:** Thirty-eight years ago (During the Great Carting).
**Material:** Standard Count House ledger paper, stained with resin and thumbprints.

I, Elias Fenn, being of sound mind but coughing up blood that tastes like pine needles, do leave my worldly possessions as follows:

* **To my eldest daughter, Mary:** I leave my heavy wool coat, my bone-handled skinning knife, and the location of my secret forage-cairn in the Amberwash (marked with three white river-stones, assuming the creek hasn't moved them).
* **To my youngest son, Tolley:** I leave my Carters' Compact token and my iron-oak walking stick. May he have better sense than to use either.
* **To the Tarrow Count House:** I leave three drops of storm-grade amber, currently held in a leather pouch around my neck. This is to settle the wheel-tax debt I incurred in Year 130, which has accrued interest. Strike my name from the ledger, Vane. I refuse to let my ghost be audited.
* **To the Keeper of Hearthwick (Hesper):** I leave one pint of rendered tallow and my apologies. I swore I would keep the lantern at the Mossy Arch trimmed last night, but the cold got into my chest and I fell asleep. I let the chain go dark. I am sorry.

**Codicil, dictated the same night, the clerk's ink barely dry:**
To Mary again, since Vane's hand was tiring and mine is worse: the cairn in the Amberwash holds nothing worth the walk unless the winter has been a hard one. Take the wool from under the flat stone and leave the rest for whoever the creek sends. And mind the third white river-stone — it stands where the ground was anchored once, long before me. There is iron-oak down that bank with tight rings, and tight rings mean a lamp stood there. Don't cut it standing. Let it fall on its own, and leave three leafy branches at the stump, as is decent.

*N.B. by Clerk Vane, at intake of goods: the pouch held three drops, storm-grade, true weight. The debt of Year 130 is struck from the roll, with interest, and the line drawn once, cleanly, per the custom of the House. No ghost will be audited on my watch.*

*From "Blood, Ink, and Timber: Legal and Architectural Records of the Reaches"*
