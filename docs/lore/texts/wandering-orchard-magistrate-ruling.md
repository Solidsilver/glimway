# Excerpt from the Tarrow Magistrate Rulings (Year 134)

**Subject:** Dispute of Property (The "Wandering Orchard" Precedent)
**Magistrate:** Senior Justice Hallet, Oak Hall
**Material:** A heavily cited parchment bound in the Count House legal archives.

**The Case:**
Farmer Codd of the Oaker Hills petitions the Hall for restitution against Farmer Fenn. During the violent Mudrise freshet of last spring, the slow drift on the southern ridge accelerated. Over the course of three nights, Codd's entire grove of mature apple trees physically drifted thirty yards to the west, crossing the recognized boundary stones and taking root entirely within Fenn's anchored sheep pasture. Fenn subsequently harvested the apples and claims ownership.

**The Ruling:**
The Hall finds in favor of Farmer Fenn. 
In the Eastern Reaches, we do not deed the coordinates of the earth; we deed the *anchor*. Codd’s failure to maintain a sufficient hearth-light on his eastern boundary allowed the Wilds to reclaim the orchard. The moment the trees slipped into the unanchored dark, they ceased to be Codd’s property and became the property of the Wilds. 

When the trees subsequently drifted into the light of Fenn’s boundary lantern, they were claimed by his anchor. Fenn is the lawful owner of the harvest. Codd is fined two coppers for wasting the Magistrate’s time with matters of poor wick-trimming.

**Subsequent Precedent (margined in the same binding):**
The Codd ruling was cited again in the matter of Hedley's smokehouse, which slipped its skids in a Haze-wick stir and came to ground below the Nine Ashes ford. The Hall again found for the holder of the nearest lit anchor, adding one line for the future justices of the Reaches: *a thing is not stolen that was first forgotten.*

*Copied into the Hearthwick archive in a Keeper's neat hand, with one note in the margin: "The Hall rules finely on whose light claims a thing, and never once on who pays for the oil to keep it lit."*

*From "Blood, Ink, and Timber: Legal and Architectural Records of the Reaches"*
