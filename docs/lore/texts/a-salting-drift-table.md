# A Salting Drift-Table, Water-Stained

**Author:** Bryony Quill
**Date:** Unclear; arrived on the spring flood-drift.
**Material:** A printed table on salt-stiffened canvas, heavily water-stained, found tangled in the reeds of the Wend.

**THE MERROW SALTINGS — DRIFT PREDICTIONS (SPRING QUARTER)**

*Tide-mark:* High Slack
*Anchor Status:* Holding.
*Drift Tendency:* Westward, toward the Reaches.

*Notes for the Watcher:*

Elara, if the flood carries this up the hill to you, know that the house is exactly where you left it.

I haven't let the window go dark. I trade two baskets of salt a week for hearth-grade amber just to keep your room anchored. The neighbors think I'm mad, paying to pin an empty room when the whole coast is riding the drift, but I don't care.

When you're ready to come down from those stubborn hills, just look for the pinprick on the waterline. I'm keeping your space perfectly still. The door is exactly where it was. Come home.

— B.

*From "The Liar's Art: Cartography and Exploration in the Reaches"*
