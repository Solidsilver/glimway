# A Scrap from the Count House Tally-Book

**Author:** Senior Clerk Vane, Tarrow Count House
**Date:** Six years ago.
**Material:** A torn strip of accounting paper, smelling faintly of pipe tobacco. Found folded inside a copy of the Carters' Compact under the North Bridge.

**Account 404: Hearthwick Train (The Brack Convoy)**

*Status:* OPEN
*Cargo Logged:* None this season.
*Tolls Paid:* None this season.

*Note for the junior clerks:* 
Do not strike Account 404 from the ledger. Yes, they are thirty years late. Yes, the Reed road is closed. But Compact Law states clearly that a train is "On the Road" until the tally-stick is surrendered at the desk and the wheel-tax is settled. We do not presume the fate of a convoy. We are accountants, not priests. Carry the balance forward to the Thaw-wick quarter. 

*(Beneath the fold, in a newer and smaller hand:)*

**Thaw-wick quarter:**
Balance carried forward, as instructed. Another wick of no cargo and no tolls. The other under-clerks have taken to calling this the ghost account, and they waste good ink complaining of it. Waste is not the Compact's way either. A train is on the road until the tally-stick is surrendered. My task is only to keep the desk ready for it. — Pell, under-clerk

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts of Hearthwick and the Lantern Road"*
