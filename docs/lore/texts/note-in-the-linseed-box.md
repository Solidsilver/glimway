# A Note Found in a Linseed Box

**Author:** Aldo Tolley
**Date:** Eight years ago.
**Material:** Scrawled on the back of a flour receipt, tucked under twenty-seven wooden foxes.

Finn,

If you are reading this, I have gone to the earth and my promise is up. 

There are twenty-seven foxes in this box. They come down the Wend every autumn, right around the first frost. They get caught in the iron grate. I clean them off and I put them in here. 

Look at the ears, Finn. Look at how they're carved. You know Silas’s work. You know this isn't his. 

I promised Wenna I would keep them safe. I promised her I would keep my mouth shut, because she was terrified of what the village would do if they knew someone was out there, throwing wood into the water. 

This box is for Wenna Hale. Not for anyone else. Give it to her. She'll say when it's time to speak. 

If Wenna is gone too... then I am sorry, my boy. You'll have to decide what a river owes a road. 

- Dad

*(On the reverse, in the same hand, smaller:)*

P.S. Clean the grate before the first frost, same as I always did. The counting is not ours to stop. 

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts of Hearthwick and the Lantern Road"*
