# Notices from the Board

**Author:** Keeper Wenna Hale, then Keeper Mara Hale
**Date:** Spanning the last three decades.
**Material:** A stack of thin, brittle notice papers, impaled on a single rusted nail from the Commons board.

*Amber-wick, 26th (Thirty-one years ago):*
**NOTICE:** Dark of Amber-wick — the outer Wilds will turn. The Sapping Grounds will shuffle. Clear your cairns. — *Keeper W. Hale*

*Leaf-wick, 4th (The day the mark was cut):*
**NOTICE:** The Lantern Road is CLOSED. The mark is cut. Do not walk past the shrine. — *Keeper W. Hale*

*Leaf-wick, 28th (Three weeks after the storm):*
**NOTICE:** Dark of Leaf-wick — the Lantern Road will turn. The Tangle will drift. I know the road is closed. I know there are no lamps to hold it. But the timetable does not stop because we are grieving. — *Keeper W. Hale*

*Smoke-wick, 14th (Twenty years ago):*
**NOTICE:** The outer Wilds will turn. The Lantern Road, though dark, will shift its course. The eastern stretches remain unanchored. — *Keeper W. Hale*

*Mud-wick, 4th (This year):*
**NOTICE:** Spring freshet. The Tangle is restless. The Lantern Road is expected to turn at midnight. As always, the road is closed. As always, we mark its turning anyway. — *Keeper M. Hale*

*From "Blood, Ink, and Timber: Legal and Architectural Records of the Reaches"*
