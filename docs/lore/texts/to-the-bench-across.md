# To the Bench-Across

**Author:** Hollis Brack
**Date:** Undated, approx. thirty-five years ago.
**Material:** Scratched onto the back of a flattened leather tally-pouch. Found in Silas’s personal toolbox.

You take the left side, neighbour,
I’ll take the right.
You watch the deep dark,
I’ll watch the light.

My knife slips out,
Your knife pulls in.
I carve the right ear long,
You carve the twin.

Two halves of a hame,
Two wheels in the rut.
You don't need a map
When the doors are shut.

You trim the wick at dusk,
I'll pour the amber slow.
Never two dark in a row—
It's all the creed we know.

Just keep the bench warm, neighbour.
I’ll drive the grey.
We’ll settle the tally
At the end of the day.

*(Beneath this, scratched lighter and slower—a different hand, older now:)*

The bench is still warm. The day is not done. The tally stands. — S.

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts of Hearthwick and the Lantern Road"*
