# The Twoford Almanac (Year 120) – Silas’s Copy

**Author:** The Astrological Faculty of Twoford University
**Annotations by:** Silas (Written in the margins with a carpenter's pencil).
**Material:** A beautifully printed, leather-bound book, heavily stained with grease, cider, and dirt.

**Printed Text:** *...by charting the lunar perigee in conjunction with the autumn equinox, the educated traveler may perfectly predict the periods of "Slack Water" within the Eastern Wilds. The Drift, being governed by tidal forces, will cease entirely during the first week of Mud-wick...*

**Silas’s Margin Note:** *Rubbish. The Drift doesn't care about the moon. It cares about who is looking at it. Turncaps face east today. The tide is pulling, moon or no moon.*

**Printed Text:** *...Travelers are advised that the "White Quiet" of winter renders the terrain perfectly stable and entirely safe for unlit traverse, as the frost locks the soil...*

**Silas’s Margin Note:** *Safe if you want to become a permanent piece of the scenery. Frost doesn't stop the woods; it just gives them teeth. Wrote this whole book from a desk, didn't you?*

**Printed Text:** *...A simple compass, shielded from the elements, is the only navigation tool a rational man requires to navigate to Hearthwick...*

**Silas’s Margin Note:** *Crossed out with three heavy, furious slashes.*
*Follow the compass if you want to drown in the Fen. Walk by stone. Walk by the lanterns. Burn this book if you run out of kindling.*

**Printed Text:** *...The prudent householder is further advised that a turncap of well-balanced copper, set above the gable, will record the prevailing airs with such regularity that a single season’s observation may be tabulated with all the certainty of the tides themselves...*

**Silas’s Margin Note:** *A turncap doesn't record. It warns. When the caps tilt east and hold, that's not weather, that's the tide pulling — count your lamps, then count your children. Carved a fox tonight while the wind made up its mind. Long ear on the left, the way I carve them. Hollis always said I did them backwards. I always told him it was him. One of us was.*

*From "The Builders and the Breakers: Tradecraft of the Reaches"*
