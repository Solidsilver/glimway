# The Hame-Polishers' List

**Author:** Anonymous (Various hands)
**Date:** Carting Day, annually for thirty years.
**Material:** A long strip of rough canvas, tacked to the inside of the Commons gate, names stitched in heavy thread.

*The Empty Hame — Polishers' Roll*

Year 1: Silas
Year 2: Silas

Year 28: Finn Tolley

Year 30: Silas

*Appended at the bottom, written in thick charcoal in Silas's unmistakable, heavy hand:*
I don't care if the leather is wearing thin. The brass still shines. Maren built the wheels they went out on, and they were good wheels. The hame goes up on the gate tomorrow. Two weaves and a break, I will be the one to polish it until it comes down.

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts of Hearthwick and the Lantern Road"*
