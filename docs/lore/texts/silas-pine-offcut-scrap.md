# Scrap of Pine Offcut

**Author:** Silas
**Date:** Undated, recent.
**Material:** A wedge of pale pine, destined for the firebox. 

*(The wood is covered in charcoal sketches of foxes. Silas has drawn the same door-fox five times. In the first four drawings, the long ear is on the right side—Hollis's style. Each of these four is violently crossed out with heavy charcoal slashes. The fifth drawing has the long ear on the left, Silas's mirror-wise style. It is not crossed out, but the charcoal lines are smudged, as if rubbed by a thumb, or a drop of water.)*

*(Written in tiny letters beneath the fifth fox):*
Not a ghost. Just late. 

*(Low on the wedge, where the firebox has already begun to scorch, a sixth fox is barely begun—two strokes of charcoal, a long left ear and the first curve of a back, drawn mid-step and heading east. There is no lintel beneath this one's feet. Instead Silas has smudged a small disc of charcoal at the horizon line: the way a lamp looks from very far off, across country that has forgotten its roads.)*

*From "Voices of the Drift: Found Texts, Vol. II"*
