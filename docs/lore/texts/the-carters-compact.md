# The Carters' Compact (Carting Day Copy)

**Author:** The Oak Hall and the Carters of the Reaches
**Date:** One hundred and fifty-five years ago.
**Material:** Heavy vellum, worn to a soft velvet at the corners from a century and a half of hands.

**THE OATH OF THE LANTERN ROAD**

Sworn this day by the Carters of Hearthwick and the Guild of Tarrow, in exchange for the safety of the anchored way. Let the wheel-tax be paid, and let the duties be kept.

**I. LIGHT**
Never shall two lamps stand dark in a row. The last cart of the train shall light the flame behind itself, and the first cart shall check the wick ahead. We buy our road with fire.

**II. LOG**
No unlogged amber shall leave the Sapping Grounds. No unmarked oak shall be hauled from the shade. Every drop and every knee shall be tallied at the Count House. We buy our trade with truth.

**III. LEAVE**
Leave the road as you would wish to find it in the dark. Cairns topped, ruts drained, gates shut against the drift.

To break this Compact is to steal the road from those who follow. A carter is on the road until their tally is settled. We ride by the light, or we do not ride at all.

*From "Blood, Ink, and Timber: Legal and Architectural Records of the Reaches"*
