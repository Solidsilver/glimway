# Eleven Days

**Author:** Master Builder Orrin
**Date:** Thirty years ago (the spring after the storm).
**Material:** A build tally, written in thick charcoal on the inside of the Stone Warden's opened chest.

Day 1: Hauled the drift-stone up the hill. My shoulders are gone.
Day 2: Cut the frame. Iron-oak. I pulled it straight from Dorrit's ruined second span. The lower joint is pinned with her oak trunnels. It flexes. It flexes exactly like she said it would.
Day 4: Hung the arms.
Day 7: Wenna brought the heart. Storm-grade amber, the size of my fist. She looked like a ghost handing it over.
Day 9: Set the stone over the frame. It wants to walk already.
Day 11: Stood it up on the path. We watched it all night so it would learn its shape.

I built it to be stubborn. I built it to keep Silas from throwing his life away in the dark.

Dorrit, if you're out there, and the stone walks toward you—don't be afraid. It's just your bridge, wearing a different coat. And it's waiting to come home.

*From "The Builders and the Breakers: Tradecraft of the Reaches"*
