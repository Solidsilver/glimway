# Lore pass: working notes

The owner's calls during the lore pass (exp/lore-final, from 2026-10-19), in the order they came.
These feed `canon.md`. When canon.md is final it supersedes this file.

Sources: `chronicle.md` (the canon so far), `drafts/glimway-lore-magic-bible.md` (the owner's new
draft), the found texts in `texts/`, and the shipped game.

## Settled

1. **The Drift is a wound, not a villain.** The forgetting was caused by someone the Dark had
   infected, long ago. The forgetting itself is not evil: the world is hurting. **The Dark is
   evil**, aware, and separate from the Drift. A biome may show the corruption plainly.
2. **People in town get personal stakes in the Dark's victims.** Elara's mother at Sallow Ford was
   only an example; it is not canon. Elara stays from the Merrow Saltings; Sallow Ford stays the
   place the four survivors reached and where Hollis keeps his lamp.
3. **The Company of the Road merges into the Carters' Compact** (its last generation). Its
   fracturing is the Weir Years, the Winter of Two Storms and the lean years after.
4. **A wick is seven days and the Turning comes every wick.** Time is compressed and the lore
   keeps the length of a year vague; the changing world makes the days hard to count.
5. **Care is strong, and oil and lamps make it stronger.** The lore must not promise that care
   alone holds land until a mechanic does. Idea, not decided: more visits or time spent at a
   place could slow its turning.
6. **New systems are canon as "later".** Practices, ranks, cross-workings, delves, bosses, boss
   branches and Bleached Hollows are recorded but not applied. No player is above level 8 yet.
7. **Hollis's lamp answers the Dark.** The Dark says nobody is coming; Hollis kept a light for a
   road nobody could walk, for thirty years.
8. **Pell's crate is old magic**: the drift gives back what was made with care. The crate was
   made long ago by a powerful, good person.
9. **The Dark is old.** It has worked through the world's history long before the Two Storms;
   Silas's fear at the fork is one small instance, not the start.
10. **The Stone Warden is the honest opposite of a binding**: it was named, not ordered, and its
    naming can be turned round. Later: attacking the Warden makes things worse.
11. **The Dark's temptation gets a plainer name** ("the Short Way", "the Easy Way" or similar),
    freeing "the Old Ways" for the paired-lamp travel working.
12. **The player's Unmoored sway is the Dark's first touch**, shared with Dark-fevered beasts.
