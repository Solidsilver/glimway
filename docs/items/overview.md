# Items: overview

Status: worked out with the owner 2026-10-05. Extends `docs/hands-on-design.md`
(sections 2–5) and stays inside the canon in `docs/lore/chronicle.md`.

Companion docs:

- `docs/items/catalogue.md`: every item by inventory tab, with source, use, wear
  and a one-line icon brief.
- `docs/items/crafting-and-repair.md`: bench and hearth recipes, fittings and wear,
  the warden-stone, village repairs, gathering, lantern posts and namings.

Numbers in all three docs are first guesses for playtesting, not tuned values.

## Principles

1. **Every item has a person behind it.** Someone made it, someone would mend it,
   and a line of canon explains why it exists. If an item has no person, it
   probably shouldn't exist yet.
2. **Wear is a story, not a chore.** Wear is slow (dozens of uses) and never
   interrupts an action. An heirloom at zero is blunt, not gone. Mending is a visit,
   not a grind.
3. **Small helps, not stats.** Keepsakes and fittings nudge (a brighter glint, a
   faint answer, a few fewer swings). There is no "+X%" in item text.
4. **The drift is the rule.** Land in lamplight remembers; land outside it forgets.
   This one rule decides what regrows, what wanders and what stays put (see "The
   drift and your things" below).
5. **Nothing punishes waiting.** No hunger, no refuelling, no expiry. Things age
   rather than vanish (fresh flowers dry; they don't rot away).
6. **In-world text stays in-world.** Item descriptions never mention real-life
   tasks, apps or experience points. Glims are the only bridge.

## Inventory tabs

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Tools** | Axes, picks, spades, buckets, the oak-mark punch, off-hand items, carry gear (satchel, apron, coat) |
| **Supplies** | Materials, fittings, repair parts, seeds and saplings, food, remedies, oils |
| **Keepsakes** | Trinkets the Wilds give back; some help from a pocket, some are for display |
| **Home goods** | Anything you place at home (furniture, decor, working pieces, seasonal pieces) |
| **Papers** | Found texts, recipe pages, naming slips |

## Three grades of tool

| Grade | Where from | Fitting slots | At zero wear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cheap** | Crafted at your bench (a few can be bought) | 1 | Breaks and is gone |
| **Heirloom** | Story only: given by a person, with a reason | 3 | Blunt or cracked until mended (at your bench, or by Orrin or Silas) |
| **Warden-set** | Any tool with a warden-stone sliver fitted | uses one of its slots | Never breaks; dulls through the day and heals overnight or on a lit tool rack |

Heirlooms don't do more by themselves. Their extra slots are what make them yours
over time. The dream tool is a three-slot heirloom carrying a sliver, a Hold
fitting and a Bite fitting.

## Carrying helps: pockets and the off hand

- **Pockets** hold keepsakes and give their help passively. A new character has
  **one pocket**. A **satchel, apron or coat** (same idea, different looks) adds a
  **second**. Pockets never go past two.
- **The off hand** opens when the character **takes a class**. It holds one visible
  carried thing: a lantern, the turncap jar, a whistle, a salve satchel. Some of
  these work only while held. **Anyone can carry anything**; each class has an
  **affinity item** that works a little better in their hands and looks a little
  different (see the catalogue).
- The off-hand item is **tucked away automatically** while you chop, dig, sit, draw
  water or fight, and comes back afterward. The player never manages it.
- So a player has at most **three helps** at once: two pockets and the off hand.

## Giving

- **Hand it over** (playing together): stand next to someone, choose an item,
  choose *Give*. They get a small line: "Wren gave you a comfrey salve." Gifts
  only: no trade screen, no haggling.
- **Send a parcel** (the other player is offline): tie items to a letter through the
  mailbox.
- **Gate shelf**: a home good by your gate on the Commons lane. You stock it;
  anyone walking past may take one. Pure gifts; no glims change hands.
- **Can't be given:** your door-fox; heirlooms (they were given to *you*). Story
  keepsakes can be **returned to their owner** (below).

### Maker's mark

Anything made at a hearth or bench carries its maker's name in its description
("Comfrey salve, made by Wren"). When someone uses a thing you made **and you aren't
nearby**, you get a quiet thank-you line in your mail. If you're together, nothing
is sent; at most a small heart shows over the maker's head.

### Recipe pages

Recipes are **Papers**. Everyone starts with three (Saltings tea, comfrey salve,
candle oil); the rest are found pages. A page can be **copied at a writing desk**
and given away. The player who collects and shares the most pages becomes the
village apothecary without anyone appointing them.

## Returning keepsakes

Some keepsakes belong to someone still in the village. Carrying one adds a quiet
line to that person's dialogue ("Is that… may I see it?"), with two answers:
**give it back** or **not yet**. "Not yet" never closes the door.

Returning is one-time and gives back a piece of the story, never something better
than the keepsake:

| Keepsake | Returned to | You get |
|---|---|---|
| Tam's knotted ox-halter | Ada | A leaf of her oil receipts, and the twins' birthday |
| Joss's tin whistle | Hazel | The oil-twists recipe page, early |
| Hollis's fox (long ear right) | Silas | No item; the conversation |

Keepsakes with no living owner in the village (Bett's, Dorrit's, Nan's) can be
**left at that person's echo camp**, and the echo settles a little softer.

## The drift and your things

| Where | What happens |
|---|---|
| **The Tangle** | Chopped trees, broken boulders and dug patches **come back** when you leave and return. |
| **Your land, inside lamplight** | Nothing regrows. A stump stays a stump; clearing is a real choice, and planting is how trees come back. Planted things **stay where you put them**. |
| **Your land, outside lamplight** | Your wild edge **regrows like the Tangle**: your own small woodlot. Things planted there **wander** a little each day (harmless, often funny). |
| **Dropped in the Tangle** | It wanders off, and may turn up later as a found thing. A warden-set tool walks itself home to your tool rack instead. |

**Gathering caps** (server-side, per area visit and per day) show as a soft line
rather than a number: *"The wood's given enough here today."* The trees nearby
quietly shuffle out of reach.

## Unmoored (a light drift status)

- **Comes from:** a stir, a turning, or long stretches in the deep Tangle.
- **Looks like:** the map goes fuzzy and the screen edges sway a little. Nothing
  else: no damage, no lost items.
- **Clears:** at once with **comfrey salve**, slowly with **willow-bark tea**, and
  **by itself** after a while back in lamplight, so the remedies help but are
  never required.

## Seasons

Seasonal home goods can be **crafted any time**, but their **materials only turn up
in their season** (walnut shells at Mudrise, madder scraps at Carting, amberfall
sap at Amberfall, frost-glass in the Quiet). Nothing expires; Bloom flowers age into
dried flowers, which are still usable and look different. Details are in
`crafting-and-repair.md`.

## Later, and open questions

- **Old ways:** storm oil and naming slips are the hooks. Two lamps given the same
  naming become one place. Nothing here should block it.
- **Gathering caps and all wear numbers** need a playtest pass.
- **Story beats for heirlooms** are suggestions in the catalogue; the quest writing
  decides the real moments.
- **Can story keepsakes be given to other players**, or only kept or returned?
  Suggested default: kept or returned only, so each one stays a single, real thing.
- **Art:** every new item needs a 16-px icon (and placed home goods a sprite); each
  catalogue entry has a one-line brief to lift into `docs/art-requests.md`.
