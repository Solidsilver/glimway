# Glimway: feature wishlist so far

*2026-10-16. Gathered from the owner's workshops with Claude, Sol and MiMo (`responses/`). It's a
wishlist, not a plan: nothing here is ordered or committed, and conflicts are kept on purpose,
marked **⚡ split**.*

**Source tags:** **(owner)** the owner said or chose it in a workshop. **[S]** Sol, **[M]** MiMo,
**[C]** Claude. Several tags mean several sources raised it.

## 1. Land, light and the turning

- **Holds engine:** chunks that keep their wick across turnings. Every plan puts this first.
  [S][M][C]
- **Lot lanterns and backland:** the woods behind your lot hold still while a lantern burns. This
  is the first bounded use of holds. [S][M]
- **Three kinds of land** [C], building on (owner) answers:
  - **Wild:** turns weekly. Some biomes always go wild when neglected, "but maybe only for certain
    biomes" (owner).
  - **Common:** paths and way-lamps that anyone can light, kept lit by presence. Social lighting
    "will help build people who build common paths together" (owner).
  - **Built:** claimed land that needs oil. Oil is the brake on claiming the whole map (owner).
- **Oil economy:** Wick Tree sap made into oil by the chandler, a store that buffers 2–3 wicks, a
  full wick of warning before anything dims, and anyone can tend anyone's lamps. [S][M]
- **Way-lamps and the Keeper's hand:** a party can hold a road outward into the turning. [M][C]
- **Trail clearing and laying that survives on held ground.** [S]
- **Clear map boundaries** showing what's held, what's common and what's turning, and an
  explanation of what survives when the light fails. [S]
- **Lasting large geography:** mountains and big lakes stay put while details turn, and biomes
  turn in different ways. ⚡ split: this changes open-map decision 18 (everything reshuffles each
  wick). Sol says prototype it first. [S]
- **Deliberately unstable regions** that transform more than the rest. [S]
- ⚡ **What happens on lapse:**
  - **Zero loss:** lapsed things return to storage; the hearth plot and lot builds last for good.
    (owner, via MiMo) [M]
  - **Dormant, then released:** built land goes dormant (overgrown), then is released after a long
    lapse, and the building packs back into the deed. [C]
  - **Reclaimed by default:** with generous warning and recovery; might become a server setting.
    (owner, via Sol) [S]
- ⚡ **The lapse clock:** calendar time or active-world time? Unresolved. Beware that one friend's
  long session could age everyone else's land. [S]

## 2. Home, building and making

- **Building tier 1, arrange:** place furniture and props freely. [M]
- **Building tier 2, compose:** a curated kit of walls, roofs, windows, floors and garden pieces,
  used on your lot and hearth plot. [M]
- **Building tier 3, paint terrain** on kept land. ⚡ split: MiMo parks it; Sol leans toward
  "Minecraft-like freedom", grown step by step, with excavation later. [M][S]
- **Wilderness hearth claims:** a modest shared home out in the Wilds, at a site you choose. ⚡
  split: Sol makes it release 2; MiMo cuts hearth H3 (deeds moving to Wilds sites) as unneeded,
  saying eight lots plus backland are enough. [S] vs [M]
- **Crafting as station chains:** gather, then mill, kiln or bench, then make. Recipes are found
  as library papers. [M]
- **Craft, then place:** every building kit piece is something somebody made. [M]
- **Restoring an authored ruin:** a found place made useful again, with local lore. [S]

## 3. Trade and economy

- **Trading both ways** (owner): resident buyers, and player stalls or a market day. [M]
- **Natural glim sinks:** buying, building and repairing, never failing to log in (owner). [M]
- **Habitica as pacing:** big play waits on real-life work, but visiting and enjoying existing
  places stays free. No repeatable activity should mint enough glims to skip the pacing. [S]
- **Pooling glims with friends** through gifts and letters (these already exist). Watch that one
  person doesn't end up funding everyone. [S]
- **Watch the faucet:** glims from XP plus gold top-ups could outrun the sinks; fees and repair
  costs are the fix. [M]

## 4. Adventure and story

- **A shared boss:** readable and server-run, needing neither caves nor chunk-crossing enemies.
  Sol brings this forward as release 3. [S]
- **An authored expedition:** one destination, a short lore trail, the boss, and a keepsake,
  recipe or tool to bring home. Reuse Dorrit's span (chapter 2) where it fits. [S]
- **Biome delves** instead of a sprawling cave system: reed-and-clay caves under lake country,
  echo-dark caves under the Wilds. Cozy at shallow depth, with optional dangerous pockets. [M]
  ⚡ split on danger: failing a delve costs time, never items [M], or real risk somewhere (owner to
  decide).
- **Drop-in authored places** in the seamless map: outposts such as Brackenwood, and ruins. [S][M]
- **Chapter 2 (Dorrit's span).** ⚡ split on timing: Sol uses it in the first expedition; MiMo
  holds story until the backbone is done and the **lore bible** is written (owner-led). [S] vs [M]
- **Complete places over sprawl:** a lake becomes worth going to through its shore, fishing,
  history, danger nearby and a reason to build there. [S]
- **Lake country as a crafting expansion:** clay, a kiln, pottery, and more kit pieces. [M]
- **Regional animals**, and later **mountains** and **tunnelling.** [S]
- **Newcomers after a story ends:** what does a newcomer find once a story project is finished?
  Retell it, or something else? [S]

## 5. Playing together

- **Async first:** the group mostly plays at different times (owner). Nothing should require
  friends to be online together. [M]
- **Shared projects** everyone adds to: the span, a road, a kiln, a restoration. [S][M]
- **Tend each other's lamps and land.** [S][M][C]
- **Loose roles without professions:** builders, makers, explorers and fighters, with no
  mandatory headcounts and nothing tied to a Habitica class. [S]
- **Witnessed turnings:** world changes that the group notices together. [M]
- **Every multiplayer feature works solo too.** [S][M]
- **Two-player lamp naming:** nice to have. [M]

## 6. Habitica

- **The Habitica mirror:** streaks and achievements shown modestly in the world. [S][M]
- **A weekly wick report** (owner: "ok, not super exciting"). [M]
- **Party goals:** back on the maybe shelf. [S][M]
- **Tie-ins celebrate, never grade.** [M]
- **The item boundary stays:** exciting loot is Glimway's own tools, materials, recipes and
  keepsakes. [S]

## 7. Standalone edition

- **A game to sell** on Steam and/or mobile: "just a fun game, no real life tie-in at all" (owner).
- **One world, two editions** that swap three edges: where glims come from, the hero, and the
  art. [C]
- **The "fun with zero XP?" test** for every new system, with the Habitica edition as the proving
  ground. [C] MiMo notes that its spine (build, craft, delve, trade) already passes it. [M]
- **Long sessions without outside time gates** in standalone (owner, via Sol), which means
  designing its own glim sources, sinks and pacing. [S]
- **Downloadable self-host binary:** a separate project, which could ship for Habitica servers
  first. [S]
- **Native clients:** deferred or cut by both planners; keep the browser client. ⚡ This sits
  against the Steam/mobile goal at some point. [S][M]

## 8. Platform and process

- **Backups and restore**, checked before persistent building ships. [S]
- **Contract freeze:** clean breaks end once the backbone lands, because the world becomes worth
  keeping. [M][S]
- **Fold felt debt into features:** the 680 ms arrival frame, gathering costs, the slow phone
  scene build, the flaky E2E tests. [M][S]
- **Docs lane one release ahead:** the lamps.md rewrite, a crafting design spike. [M]
- **One player promise per release**, with a short acceptance playtest. [S][M]
- **Ask friends** what they chose to do, where they stopped and what they wanted next. [S]
- **Bring back the step-back review** (0.10 and 0.11 were missed). [M]
- **Cleanup:** delete plan.md's release table, and fix or fold build-status.md and lamps.md. [M]
- **Backburner (owner, 2026-10-21): the world lab inside the dev panel,** as a diagnostic tool rather
  than a lab of its own: in dev mode it would stand in for the map. The hard part is fitting it in
  without crowding the panel. (owner)

## 10. The big splits, in one line each

1. **Spine:** discovery and a world carrying its players' history [S], shared making [M], or social
   light over three land tiers [C].
2. **Order:** thin slices (holds, then a home, then an expedition) [S], or the whole backbone
   before any content [M] (owner agreed in MiMo's workshop).
3. **Loss:** reclaimed by default [S, owner via Sol], zero loss [M, owner via MiMo], or dormant
   then released [C].
4. **Terrain:** lasting large geography [S], or everything turns, as now [M][C].
5. **Wilds homes:** yes, in release 2 [S], or cut [M].
6. **Story timing:** early, inside the expedition [S], or after the backbone and lore bible [M].
