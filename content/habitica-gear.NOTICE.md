# Notice: habitica-gear.json

`habitica-gear.json` is a snapshot of numbers and names from Habitica's game content: gear stats,
class tags, the English gear names, and the lists of pets, mounts and appearances. It was fetched
from Habitica's public `GET /api/v3/content` endpoint (the revision is recorded in the file's
`provenance`), flattened, and stripped of all descriptions and other text; only the English names
(`text`) are kept. No numbers were changed.
